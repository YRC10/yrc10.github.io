I am a PhD student in the School of Information Sciences at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://illinois.edu/) advised by [Dr. Dong Wang](https://www.wangdong.org/).

Prior to this, I received a Bachelor of Applied Science with Distinction in Electrical Engineering and a Master of Engineering in Electrical and Computer Engineering, both from the [University of British Columbia](https://www.ubc.ca/).

My research interests lie in AI for Social Good and Human-Centered AI, with a particular focus on social sensing approaches that leverage multimodal digital traces to understand societal patterns and human behavior.
